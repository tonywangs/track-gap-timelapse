Data provenance & limitations

These inputs are labeled synthetic by the manifest author. Interpolation is a linear coordinate assumption, not evidence of a route, speed, or continuous tracking. Missing times break continuity. Imported names, links, elevation, metadata, and extensions are not used. Source SHA-256 hashes, diagnostic locations, and all gap intervals are preserved in the timeline JSON.

Do not record or distribute anyone’s location without their consent. This synthetic workflow has not established compatibility with GPS providers. Human usability and browsers other than the tested Chromium version remain unvalidated.
